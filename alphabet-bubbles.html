alphábētos, the alphabet

Learn the 24 letters of Attic Greek by shooting bubbles.

A letter is named at the top of the screen. Aim and shoot the bubble that shows it. A wrong shot sticks to the board, and every third miss drops a new row. Clear the board before the bubbles reach the line.

    The Attic alphabet

      LetterNameSound

      Sigma is written ς at the end of a word and σ everywhere else. The sounds given are the reconstructed pronunciation of classical Athens, so θ, φ and χ are breathy t, p and k, not the th, f and ch of modern Greek. Bubble colours follow the sound classes above.
